Scope3Bridge

A full-stack platform helping small and medium suppliers respond to sustainability data requests from the much larger companies they sell to — the kind of request that currently means a scramble through spreadsheets and an accountant's best guess. Grounded in the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard for Scope 1/2/3 emissions, it turns raw utility bills into a structured CO2e calculation and a client-ready report. Backend built solo on Node.js, Express, and MySQL; the React frontend and CO2e engine are in active development.

SmartFactory Micro-Monitor

Enterprise ESG monitoring hardware costs upwards of €15,000 a year — out of reach for most small manufacturers. I built a working alternative for €80: a Raspberry Pi 3B station wired to six sensors that continuously tracks temperature, humidity, air quality, dust, noise, and energy draw on a factory floor. Breaches trigger an LED and buzzer in real time, every reading is logged to SQLite, and a Flask dashboard renders live charts with one-click PDF compliance reports. As lead developer I handled everything from GPIO wiring and I2C debugging to the full five-layer software architecture.
